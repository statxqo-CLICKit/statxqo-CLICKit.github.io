# RX7 VAPOR — by Racer X

RX7 VAPOR is a wallet-safety dApp: paste any wallet address and it scans balances, tokens, and the approvals that let other apps move your money across major chains, then flags drainers and scam approvals so you can revoke them.

**Live:** https://statxqo-clickit.github.io/rx7-vapor/

## Safety
- **Read-only.** It reads public on-chain data only and never signs a transaction for you.
- **It will never ask for your seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private keys.** Anyone who asks for them is scamming you.
- Any transaction (such as buying the Access Pass) is shown in your own wallet for you to review and approve.

## Pricing

- **Access Pass: $9.99 one-time, paid in USDC on Base** (unlimited scans + cleanup actions), paid from your own wallet to `0x20dc007398F2748Ffb1618D69be0D72798a259F2`.
