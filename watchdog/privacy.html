# Watchdog Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 14, 2026 · Developer: C.L.I.C.K.it_

Watchdog is a security checker that runs entirely on your device.

**What we collect:** Nothing. Watchdog does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal or device data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash-reporting services.

**No internet access:** Watchdog does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone.

**What the app reads on your device, and why:**
- The list of installed apps, their permissions, install source and signing certificates — to find risky, hidden, tampered or spyware-like apps.
- The device's trusted CA certificate store — to warn you about user-installed certificates that could intercept traffic.
- Device settings such as screen lock, encryption, security patch level, developer options, Private DNS, VPN and proxy status — to calculate your Security Score.
- App storage sizes, only if you grant Usage Access — to show cache sizes.

**What is stored:** A list of app signing-certificate fingerprints and your last scan result, kept in the app's private storage on your device so Watchdog can alert you when something changes. It is removed when you uninstall Watchdog. App backup is disabled.

**Sharing:** A scan report leaves your device only if you tap Export and choose an app to share it with. That transfer is controlled by you and the app you choose.

**Children:** Watchdog is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
