4c. Market

Players can list cards for a fixed price or a timed auction. Listed cards are held by the market contract until they sell, the auction is settled, or the seller cancels (auctions can be cancelled only before the first bid). Each sale pays a 2.5% platform fee and the 5% creator royalty, with the rest to the seller, all in the same transaction. Outbid bidders are refunded automatically. Bids in the last 5 minutes extend the auction by 5 minutes. Prices are set by players; FOMO does not set, guarantee or support any card price.
